[Adjustments and Optimizations]
✦Resonators & Weapons✦
●Resonator Animations
Optimized certain animations for Resonators:
- Improved the animations for turning around in place and transitions between walking and running.
- Improved the animation for taking hits in combat.
- Following the Version 3.8 update, these animations will appear more natural, and transitions between movements will flow more seamlessly.
- Animations may vary slightly depending on the Resonator's body type.
 
●Rover's Resonance Conversion
Optimized Rover's Resonance Conversion:
- Upgraded the UI of the Resonance Conversion screen.
- Added Echo set auto-switch. After Rover's Resonance Attribute is converted, the equipped Echo set will now be automatically switched to the one for the corresponding Resonance Attribute.
- If no Echo set has been selected for a specific Attribute, you will be prompted to make the selection.
- New display order. Newly obtained Resonators and the Focus Resonator set via the Resonator Ascension Planner will now be displayed first in the list on the right side of the Resonators screen.
- Optimized the Outfit Preview feature. When previewing outfits on the Preview screen (accessed via Resonators or Store), you can now drag and move around the splash art on the screen.
 
✦Features✦
●Echo Backpack
Optimized Echo Backpack and Data Bank:
- Optimized the filtering logic in Batch Management for Echoes. Changing filter options will now clear any currently selected Echoes.
- Adjusted default sorting in Batch Management. Echoes will now be sorted by Rarity by default.
- When the weekly Targeted Merge attempts reach 0, you will now automatically exit the Targeted Merge screen and return to the Data Bank screen.
- Standardized the threshold reminder popups for Smart Discard and Data Merge of the Echo Backpack. A prompt will now appear whenever your occupied Echo slots exceed 2,950.
 
- Optimized the performance of certain Selfie Expressions in Selfie Mode of the Camera.
 
✦Leveling✦
●Blitz Mode
Added Blitz Mode:
- You can enable or disable Blitz Mode when claiming rewards in Simulation Challenge, Forgery Challenge, Tacet Suppression, Boss Challenge, or Weekly Challenge.
- When Blitz Mode is on, restarting a challenge after claiming rewards no longer requires reloading.
 
●Resonator Ascension Planner
Optimized the functionality of the Resonator Ascension Planner:
- Added to the Resonator Ascension Planner page an Overview tab under which information about the selected Resonator is displayed.
- In addition to standard info, the Overview screen also displays a Recommended Lineup section. (※Recommended Lineup info comes from Wuthering Waves' Official Guide Depot.)
- A Material Locator button is added to the Material Collection page beside the Challenge button, allowing you to locate unlocked challenges that drop the required materials. Press the button to locate and Fast Travel to the corresponding material collection challenge on the map.
※The Recommended Lineup feature is currently in testing and subject to changes.
 
✦Challenges, Puzzles, & Events✦
●Weekly Activity:
Made certain gameplay mechanics in Phantasma Dreamland: Rhapsody more balanced:
- Adjusted the effects of the following Relics in "Phantasma Dreamland: Rhapsody": Amorphous Photonic Tree, Misaligned Instrument, Cat-Peeping Scope.
- Here are the effects after the adjustments:
①Amorphous Photonic Tree: Echoes on a non-blank Dream Block additionally gain Dreamland Coupons equal to 20 x (Relic Level)% of their Daily Coupon yield during settlement.
Whenever an Echo on the field Removes itself or triggers Gradation, there is a {Relic Level}% + 10% chance to randomly change the Echo's Dream Block.
Level Up: The Relic will automatically level up for every (Relic Level) time/times a Dream Block changes.
②Misaligned Instrument: When Dream of Integration Echoes are devoured, they trigger their Integration effect and will generate 1 copy of itself on blank blocks. The Daily Coupon yield of the generated Echoes is increased by (Relic Level)%.
Level Up: The Relic will automatically level up for every 3 times Integration happens.
③Cat-Peeping Scope: Dream of Exchange Echoes will be considered Dream of Integration Echoes. The number of Echoes allowed to undergo Integration is increased by (Relic Level).
Echoes being integrated have a 20% chance to generate 1 copy of themselves, whose Daily Coupon yield is increased by 5 x (Relic Level);
There is a 20% chance to generate 1 Dream Block: Ice Shards every day;
Level Up: The Relic will automatically level up for every (Relic Level) Echo/Echoes removed.
 
- Optimized some pre-combat mechanics in certain challenges. In Tower of Adversity, Whimpering Wastes, and Endstate Matrix, you can now switch between Resonators in your current team before interacting with the mechanism to start the challenge.
- When you enter a Tower of Adversity challenge, the Environment Report will no longer automatically pop up. To view the Environment Report, please press the button in the upper-left corner of the screen.
- Optimized the tracking detection radius for Thousandfold Petals in certain Dream Fox Statue puzzles.
- Optimized transition animations when entering and exiting the Inner Threshold.
 
✦UI✦
- A Material Locator button is added to the Material Collection page of Guidebook beside the Challenge button, allowing you to locate unlocked challenges that drop the required materials. Press the button to locate and Fast Travel to the corresponding material collection challenge on the map.

✦Graphics & Performance✦
●Graphics Settings
Added a Graphics Quality option for some Console devices:
- Following the Version 3.8 maintenance update, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X will feature a Graphics Quality option, allowing players to toggle between Performance and Quality.
- If your device meets the requirements, you can enable/adjust this option via Settings > Graphics > Graphics Quality. You can adjust the Graphics Quality settings based on your needs for better performance and visual fidelity.
※This feature is not supported on PS5 and Xbox Series S.
 
Added PSSR (Spectral Super Resolution) support for some Console devices:
- Following the Version 3.8 maintenance update, the PS5 Pro will support PSSR (Spectral Super Resolution), which can be toggled via Settings > Graphics > PSSR.
- Enable PSSR to improve overall graphic clarity and quality (up to a display resolution of 4K).
 
Adjusted the Render Distance option on PC:
- The three-preset (Default, Far, and Farthest) Render Distance option on PC has been replaced with a slider scale ranging from 1 to 10, where higher values correspond to greater render distances.
 
✦Audio✦
- Optimized audio performance of voiced storylines. When playing voiced storylines, advancing to the next line of dialogue before the current line finishes playing will now cause a fade-out effect to be added between the two lines.

✦Online Testing✦
●Limited Test
Categorized Resource Downloads and Resource Cleanup on PC:
- Categorized Resource Downloads Overview: When downloading game resources via the PC launcher, in addition to the Tiered Client Resource Downloads options—UHD, HD, and SD—you can choose between two package sizes: Full and Basic. Select Full to download all available environment assets in one go, and you only have to download small quantities of sources (unwatched cutscenes) based on your account progress after you enter the game; or select Basic to download only the resources required to launch the game, and you need to download the necessary environment assets and cutscenes based on your account progress after you enter the game, while the remaining resources will be downloaded in the background while you play.
- Resource Cleanup Overview: When your account contains cutscenes that have been watched and environment/audio assets that are no longer needed, you can go to Settings > Other > Resource Cleanup to check the size of the cleanable files and choose to remove them to free up disk space.
- Eligibility & Schedule: PC (※Official Launcher): We recently, during Version 3.7, made a PC launcher update available to some of the Rovers eligible for the test, and have started the limited test for the aforementioned features.
※The feature will also be gradually made available on PC platforms with non-official launchers after the test concludes. Please stay tuned for announcements on these platforms.
※The test schedule may be subject to change due to version stability or other considerations. For more details on the test, please refer to the upcoming official announcements.
※This feature is at the testing stage and is not the final version.
